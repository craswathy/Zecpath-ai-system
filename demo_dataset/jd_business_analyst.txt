Job ID: CX-BA-001

Title: Business Analyst

Company: Concentrix

Domain Category: Business Intelligence / Data Analytics

Location: Mexico City, Mexico

POSITION OVERVIEW

Concentrix is seeking a Business Analyst to support the Global Sales Center COE by developing business intelligence reporting, analyzing sales performance data, supporting business planning, and driving process improvements.

REQUIREMENTS

Experience: 2–4+ years of experience as a Business Analyst, Reporting Analyst, Sales Analyst, or Business Intelligence Analyst.

Education: Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Statistics, Economics, Data Analytics, Information Systems, or Engineering.

Technical Skills: Advanced Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Dashboards, Reporting Automation), SQL, Tableau, Microsoft Power BI, Power Platform Solutions, Microsoft Applications.

Analytics & Domain Knowledge: Sales performance analysis, quota attainment tracking, business planning support, trend analysis, data cleansing, performance management.

Communication & Professional Skills: Advanced PowerPoint skills, stakeholder management, cross-functional collaboration, multi-project coordination, English and Spanish fluency.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Develop and maintain business intelligence reports, dashboards, and performance tracking tools for sales performance and quota attainment.

Collect, cleanse, and analyze sales performance data from multiple sources, identifying trends, opportunities, and risks.

Support monthly and quarterly business reviews by providing data-driven insights to sales leadership and CRM teams.

Drive process improvement initiatives and manage multiple ad hoc reporting requests simultaneously.
