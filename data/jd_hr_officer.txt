Job ID: PX-HRO-001
Title: HR Officer
Company: PX.com
Domain Category: Human Resources / People Operations
Location: Casco Viejo, Panama City, Panama
Employment Type: Full-time

POSITION OVERVIEW
PX.com is hiring an HR Officer to support day-to-day people operations, employee engagement initiatives, recruitment coordination, and office management across global teams.

REQUIREMENTS
- Experience: 1–5+ years of experience in HR operations, people coordination, office management, or administrative support roles.
- Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or related fields.
- Technical & HRIS Skills: Experience using HRIS systems (BambooHR preferred), Google Workspace, MS Office, Slack, and digital collaboration tools.
- Core HR Competencies: Onboarding, offboarding, HR documentation, candidate screening, interview coordination, employee engagement, internal event planning, office management.
- Communication & Collaboration: Strong English communication skills, cross-departmental collaboration (HR, IT, Marketing), ability to manage multiple priorities in an international environment.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Coordinate end-to-end onboarding and offboarding, system access setup, equipment provisioning, and HR documentation.
- Support recruitment workflows by screening resumes, scheduling candidate interviews, and maintaining candidate updates.
- Plan and execute internal employee engagement events, team lunches, and culture initiatives.
- Maintain an organized, well-functioning office environment and collaborate with IT and Marketing teams.
