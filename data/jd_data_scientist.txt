Title: Data Scientist
Company: QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey
Domain Category: Data Science / Artificial Intelligence
Location: São Paulo, Brazil

POSITION OVERVIEW
QuantumBlack, AI by McKinsey, is looking for a Data Scientist to join our multidisciplinary team. You will solve high-impact business problems, build scalable data science products, and apply machine learning, statistics, and mathematics to client challenges.

REQUIREMENTS
Education: Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, Physics, Data Science, or Engineering.
Programming Languages: Practical coding experience in at least two of the following: Python, R, Scala, SQL.
Data Science & ML Skills: Strong foundation in data science concepts, machine learning modeling, statistical analysis, and writing efficient code.
Problem-Solving & Analytics: Analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and the ability to work with complex datasets in digital/analytics transformations.
Communication & Teamwork: Strong verbal and written communication skills in English (Portuguese is a plus); ability to present findings to colleagues and clients.

RESPONSIBILITIES
Collaborate with cross-functional teams of data scientists, engineers, and designers to build scalable analytics products.
Apply mathematics, statistics, and machine learning to real-world datasets across operations, marketing, and strategy domains.
Contribute to internal data science toolboxes and reusable code repositories.
Present insights, models, and data-driven recommendations clearly to team members and client stakeholders.
