About the job
About PayJoy

PayJoy, a Public Benefit Corporation, is a mission-first credit provider dedicated to helping under-served customers in emerging markets to achieve financial stability and success. Our patented technology for secured credit provides an on-ramp for new customers to enter the credit system. Through PayJoy’s point-of-sale financing and card offerings, customers gain access to a modern quality of life. PayJoy’s credit also allows our customers to seize opportunities as micro-entrepreneurs, and acts as insurance for tough times. Through our cutting-edge machine learning, data science, and anti-fraud AI, we have served over 18 million customers as of 2025 while achieving solid profitability for sustainable growth.

This role

We're looking for a Data Analyst who combines data analysis, CRM administration, and process automation skills. The ideal candidate enjoys turning scattered data into actionable insights and automating manual workflows using low-code. This person will work closely with the commercial, operations and fraud teams as well as cross-functional stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities

•	Partner with the commercial team to understand reporting needs and translate them into dashboards, KPIs, and actionable insights ( SQL, Python). 
•	Act as a key point of contact for cross-functional stakeholders (sales, operations, finance) requesting data analysis or process automation support. 
•	Manage and optimize the CRM (Zoho CRM): modules, custom fields, workflows, assignment rules, and data quality. 
•	Clean, validate, and normalize data (names, phone numbers, emails, geolocation) to ensure data quality across merchant/customer databases. 
•	Present findings and recommendations to commercial leadership and other stakeholders in a clear, actionable format. 
•	Perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned by the department, based on evolving business needs. 

Requirements


•	Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Industrial Engineering, Data Science, Economics, or a related field. 
•	2+ years of experience in data analysis, business operations, or CRM-related roles. 
•	Intermediate-to-advanced SQL skills (Snowflake, BigQuery, or similar preferred). 
•	Experience administering a CRM (Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Salesforce, or other). 
•	Advanced Google Sheets/Excel skills (formulas, pivot tables, data validation). 
•	Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to work with ambiguous or incomplete data. 
•	Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate technical concepts for non-technical, commercial-facing stakeholders. 
•	Ability to manage multiple priorities and collaborate effectively across departments. 
•	Nice to have: basic Python, BI tools (Hex, Looker, Power BI) or exposure to fintech/retail environments with field sales operations.
