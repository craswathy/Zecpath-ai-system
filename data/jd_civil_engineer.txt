POSITION OVERVIEW
Vestal Corporation is seeking a Civil Engineer in Guadalajara, Mexico to perform civil site design, roadway geometry, commercial design, and water and sewer infrastructure projects while collaborating with multidisciplinary teams and clients.

REQUIREMENTS
- Experience: Minimum 3 years of experience in civil engineering, commercial design, and site infrastructure projects.
- Education: Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering or equivalent.
- Licensing: Registered Professional Engineer (P.E.) preferred.
- Mandatory Codes Knowledge: Functional knowledge of ASCE, AWWA, and International Building Codes (IBC).
- Technical Software: Proficient user of AutoCAD and Civil 3D strongly preferred.
- Core Engineering Competencies: Site layout and design, grading, stormwater drainage, sanitary sewer design, erosion control, flood studies, pavement/roadway design.
- Interpersonal & Soft Skills: Excellent written and verbal communication, client interfacing, cross-disciplinary collaboration, problem-solving, quality control compliance, time management.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Perform site layout and design, grading, stormwater drainage, sanitary sewer systems, erosion control, and flood studies.
- Develop pavement and roadway design for industrial and commercial projects using AutoCAD and Civil 3D.
- Coordinate with internal engineering disciplines, external partners, clients, and vendors for seamless project execution.
- Maintain civil design/engineering standards and comply with corporate Quality Control and Project Management procedures.
