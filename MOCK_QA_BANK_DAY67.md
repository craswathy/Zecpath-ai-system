# Mock Stakeholder Q&A -- Day 67

Ask these cold. Score each answer: Clear (short, honest, specific), Vague
(true but waffly), or Weak (wrong, defensive or no answer). Answers live in
the deck's backup slide, the Day 66 demo script and the interview Q&A docs.

## Recruiter
1. How does this save me time?
2. Can I trust a rejection recommendation?
3. What do I see for a candidate I disagree with?

## Hiring manager
4. How accurate is it compared with my team's judgment?
5. What happens if the system fails halfway?
6. Why did this candidate score lower than that one?

## Technical lead
7. Why rules and sentence-transformers, not a custom trained model?
8. How would this scale to 10,000 resumes a day?
9. What is real in this demo and what is simulated?

## Statistician or auditor
10. How were the weights and thresholds chosen, and how do you know they generalize?
11. How would you check the scores for bias?
12. What would you need to validate it on real data?

## Rules for the session
- Answer in 30 seconds or less, then stop.
- If you do not know, say "I haven't measured that, here is how I would."
- Never claim a number you cannot reproduce from the repo.
